VALIDATION SCOPE
The official source identifies the sound as a single small cat meow. The WAV
was decoded successfully and its waveform and spectrogram inspected: audible-
range energy is present, with a natural nonuniform envelope and harmonic
structure. The file is non-silent and not clipped.

available audio-output path explicitly reported that audio input is unsupported.
Waveform inspection and source descriptions do not establish that a listener
will recognize the sound. A human or audio-capable playtest should verify the
meow and its balance against music, voices, and combat effects before claiming
that subjective requirement is tested.
