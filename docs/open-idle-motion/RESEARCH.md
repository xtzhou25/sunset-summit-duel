# Open skeletal idle motion research

## Recommended: ACCAD Open Motion Project

Official page explicitly supplies BVH skeletal motion and CC BY 3.0 terms. The original Male 2 ZIP was actually downloaded, read and selected clips extracted. E8 Bounce is the strongest fighting-idle starting point (227 frames, about 7.53 s at 30 Hz); A1 Stand (115 frames, 3.80 s) and A2 Sway (1280 frames, 10.66 s at 120 Hz) supply grounded natural-motion alternatives. The D15/D16 stance-switch files are useful only if an appropriate stationary portion can be trimmed; they should not force stance changes into idle. A real source segment must be retargeted, not merely cited while authoring sinusoidal offsets.

## Alternative: Quaternius Universal Animation Library

Official creator page and itch listing state CC0, humanoid rig, FBX/GLB, ready for retargeting and commercial use. The Standard ZIP is free (15 MB), while Pro and editable Blend Source variants require payment. This is an authored game-animation library, not verified raw human mocap. Exact free combat-idle clip was not downloaded or verified during this bounded research; do not promise a specific included free clip or treat paid source as available.

Sources: https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html and https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library

## Conditional alternative, not selected: CMU Graphics Lab

Official source says motions are free for all uses and may be included in commercially sold products, but may not be resold directly even in converted form. This is a custom permission statement, NOT CC0. Because the project returns editable reusable source, ACCAD CC BY is a clearer fit. CMU root/search/FAQ direct opening timed out; the official indexed homepage returned its license wording. No CMU asset was downloaded or used.

Source: https://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/

## Rejected shortcuts

Mixamo free access is not an open-source-data license. Bandai Namco CC BY-NC-ND motion is not suitable for a retargeted distributable animation. Unverified third-party reuploads and AI model motions with unclear underlying dataset rights were not used.
